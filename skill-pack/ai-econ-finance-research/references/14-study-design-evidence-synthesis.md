## Minimum Gates

For original-data studies, establish the research owner, ethics/data rules, construct, target population, assignment or sampling unit, unit of analysis, intervention/measurement timing, power inputs, and whether the plan is prospective, blinded, or retrospective. Never present AI output as ethics approval.

For systematic reviews, establish the protocol, databases and search boundary, frozen eligibility criteria, independent screening/adjudication, report-version families, extraction provenance, target estimand, effect dependence, and conceptual poolability. PRISMA improves reporting but does not certify review quality.

## Default Output Contract

Return:

1. proposed design/protocol and assumptions;
2. unresolved researcher decisions;
3. usable artifact or ledger schema;
4. failure modes and stop conditions;
5. concrete verification steps;
6. what remains exploratory or uncertain.
